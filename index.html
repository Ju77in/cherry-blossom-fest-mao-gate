Venue & Route Guide

Venue Location: Cultural Ground, Mao Gate, Senapati District, Manipur — 795150.

Transit Route: Located along the Asian Highway 1 / National Highway 2 (NH-2). Easy road access from Kohima (~30 km south) and Senapati Town / Imphal (~105 km north).

Visitor Facilities: Designated parking zones for light passenger vehicles, on-site drinking water stations, eco-friendly food hubs, and clean sanitation blocks.
